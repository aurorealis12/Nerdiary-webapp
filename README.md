# Nerdiary

> Working title. A personal, online library for everything you watch, read, play and listen to.

Nerdiary is a web app that helps you keep track of movies, books, games and music: what you have finished, what you are in the middle of, and what you want to get to next. You can also share your picks with friends and get recommendations back.

This is a team project developed by computer science students as part of a university course.
